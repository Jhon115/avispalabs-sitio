Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 · Habeas Data

Personal Data Policy

This is a courtesy translation. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

At AvispaLabs we take care of your personal data. This policy explains what data we receive, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights, under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and other Colombian data protection rules.

1. Data controller

Jhon Jaider Giraldo García, an individual operating under the brand AvispaLabs and its lines Avispa Flow and Avispa Studio, domiciled in Bugalagrande, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. Email: jhon@avispalabs.com. Website: avispalabs.com.

2. What data we receive

  • Contact data you give us when you write to us: name, business name, WhatsApp number, email and the content of your message.
  • Data needed to provide the service: material you send us (videos, photos, logos, price lists, inventories) and billing or payment data.
  • Minimal technical data: like any website, our hosting provider and the libraries the page uses may log your IP address and browser type for security and operation.

The contact form on this website does not store your data in a database: it only builds a message that you decide to send by WhatsApp or email.

3. What we use your data for

  • Answering your questions and sending you quotes.
  • Providing the contracted services (automations, WhatsApp assistants, video editing) and giving you support.
  • Coordinating payments, deliveries and invoicing, and complying with legal obligations.
  • Sending you information about new services or packages, only if you authorize it. You can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Data of our clients' customers (Avispa Flow)

When we install a WhatsApp assistant for your business, messages from your customers pass through the system. In that case your business is the data controller and AvispaLabs acts as the data processor: we only use that data to make the service work, following your instructions, and we do not sell it or use it for other purposes.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. We only share it with providers needed to operate, who may be outside Colombia (international transfer or transmission): WhatsApp / Meta (messaging), Zoho (email), GitHub (hosting of this website), artificial intelligence and server providers we use to deliver Avispa Flow, and financial institutions for payments. We will also hand it to authorities when the law requires it.

6. Your rights

As the data subject you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your data.
  • Request proof of the authorization you gave us.
  • Know how we have used your data.
  • Revoke the authorization or ask us to delete your data, when there is no legal or contractual duty to keep it.
  • File complaints with Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC). Under article 16 of Law 1581 of 2012, you must first submit your inquiry or claim to us and complete that process, within the time limits in section 7. You may go to the SIC if those time limits expire without a reply or if the reply does not resolve your request.
  • Access your data free of charge.

7. How to exercise your rights

Write to jhon@avispalabs.com with your name, what you need and a way to reach you.

  • Inquiries: we reply within 10 business days at most (extendable by 5 more business days, telling you why).
  • Claims (correct, update, delete or revoke): we reply within 15 business days at most (extendable by 8 more business days, telling you why).

8. Authorization

By writing to us through the form, WhatsApp or email, and checking the acceptance box, you authorize us to process your data under this policy. Sensitive data (if ever needed) is only processed with your express authorization, and you are not required to provide it.

9. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your data (encrypted connections, password-protected access, well-known providers). We keep data for as long as the business relationship lasts and for the time the law requires (for example, accounting and tax rules); after that we delete it.

10. Minors

Our services are aimed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Rules you can consult

  • Law 1581 of 2012: personal data protection
  • Decree 1377 of 2013: regulates Law 1581
  • Decree 1074 of 2015: Single Decree for the Trade, Industry and Tourism sector
  • Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC)

12. Changes and effective date

This policy is effective as of November 7, 2026. If we change it in any important way, we will publish it on this page.
