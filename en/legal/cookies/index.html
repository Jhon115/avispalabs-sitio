Cookie Policy

This is a courtesy translation. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

This page explains what cookies are and what avispalabs.com actually uses.

1. What are cookies?

They are small files some websites save in your browser to remember things, measure visits or show advertising.

2. What does this website use?

  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, nothing like that.
  • We use temporary technical browser storage (sessionStorage) only to make the honeycomb page-change animation work. It is deleted when you close the tab and does not identify you.
  • We save in your browser (localStorage) that you already accepted the privacy notice and which language you chose, so we don't ask every time. It does not identify you.
  • The Avispa Studio price calculator checks the day's exchange rate at open.er-api.com; that request sends no data about you beyond the technical data of any connection.
  • To load fonts and animations we use external services (Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg). As on any website, those services may log technical data such as your IP to deliver the files. You can read their own policies on their sites.
  • When you tap the WhatsApp or email buttons you leave our website: the policies of WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider apply there.

3. How to control them

You can delete or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings. The website will keep working; it may just appear without the transition animation.

4. If this changes

If we ever add measurement or advertising tools, we will ask for your permission with a notice before turning them on and will update this page.
